How I work

From problem to product

The same four steps run through every project above.

Step 1

Find the real person

A parent at the bus stop, a broker chasing renewals, a commuter who dozes off. I start with who is stuck and why.

Step 2

Test the assumptions

Check claims against data before building anything, the way I did with the hiring pipeline audit.

Step 3

Build something real

A working product people can click through, not a slide. It moves the conversation forward faster.

Step 4

Connect the systems

Wire it into the tools the business already runs on, like CRM and support desks, so it survives day one.
